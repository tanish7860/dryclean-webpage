Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


        1. What services do you offer?
        We provide professional dry cleaning, laundry, steam ironing, and stain removal services. We also offer pickup
        and delivery for your convenience.

        2. How does the pickup and delivery service work?
        You can schedule a pickup through our website or by calling us. Our delivery team will collect your clothes,
        clean them, and return them to you at the scheduled time.

        3. How long does it take to get my clothes back?
        Most orders are completed within 24 to 48 hours. However, special items or bulk orders may take a bit longer.

        4. Do you remove all types of stains?
        We try our best to remove all stains using professional cleaning agents and techniques. However, some stains
        (like ink, rust, or dye transfer) may not be completely removable without risking fabric damage.

        5. What if my garment gets damaged or lost?
        We take utmost care of every item. In the rare case of damage or loss, we offer compensation up to 10 times the
        cleaning charge of the item, as per our Terms and Conditions.

        6. Do you clean delicate fabrics like silk or wool?
        Yes, we specialize in cleaning delicate and designer garments using fabric-specific methods to ensure the best
        care.

        7. How should I prepare my clothes for pickup?
        Please check your pockets for valuables (coins, keys, etc.) and separate heavily stained or delicate items if
        possible.

        8. What payment methods do you accept?
        We accept cash, UPI, and online payments at the time of delivery or pickup.

        9. What happens if I don’t pick up my clothes on time?
        Items not collected within 30 days may be donated or disposed of, as mentioned in our Terms and Conditions.
